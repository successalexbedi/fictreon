Chapter One

The Bastard Daughter

The ash fell like snow over the ruined capital of Vael. It had been three days since the last emperor's pyre had burned itself out, and the city still smelled of smoke and old blood. Elara Vane pulled the hood of her cloak tighter, obscuring her face from the patrolling guards who moved through the streets with the rigid precision of men who had something to prove.

She kept to the shadows, her boots silent on the cobblestones. She had learned long ago that survival was not about strength or speed. It was about being forgettable. It was about being the kind of person whose face no one bothered to remember.

The tavern at the end of the alley was called The Broken Crown. The irony was not lost on her. She pushed open the heavy wooden door and stepped inside, letting the warmth of the hearth wash over her. The room was sparse, filled with the dregs of a fallen kingdom—mercenaries, merchants, and the occasional noble who had fallen far enough to drink alongside them.

She took a seat in the farthest corner, her back to the wall, and ordered a cup of watered wine. She didn't drink it. She just held it, watching the door, waiting.

He arrived just as the bell tower struck midnight. He was tall, cloaked in gray, with the kind of face that had seen too many winters and not enough mercy. He didn't look like a priest. He looked like a soldier who had traded his sword for a scripture.

He sat down across from her without asking permission. "You're hard to find, Elara Vane."

She didn't flinch. She had expected this. She had prepared for this. "And you're not hard to spot, Ash-priest. You smell like a funeral."

A ghost of a smile touched his lips. "A funeral I intend to prevent. The Ashborn Order knows who you are. They know what you carry in your blood. They will not stop until you are dead, or until you take what is rightfully yours."

Elara set her cup down. The wine rippled. "I don't want a crown."

"It doesn't matter what you want," he said quietly. "The crown is a debt. And you, my lady, are already in arrears."
