The Ashen Crown is the opening chapter of the Ashborn Saga — a slow-burn political fantasy that trades prophecy for pragmatism and chosen ones for pragmatists. Alex Bedi's debut follows Elara Vane, a bastard daughter of a fallen dynasty who has spent her life hiding from the very throne she was born to inherit.

When an ash-priest discovers her identity, Elara must decide between the anonymity that has kept her alive and the crown that could either save or destroy everything she loves.

The pacing is deliberate and the payoff is massive. Elara is the kind of protagonist you spend the first 100 pages not being sure you like — and by the end you'd burn a city for her. Bedi doesn't fall into the chosen-one trap. Every conversation has weight, every betrayal lands, and the ash-priests are genuinely terrifying in a way most fantasy villains never manage.
